# Roblox thumbnail market benchmark — 15 November 2026

## What changed in the market

| Period | Evidence | Implication for thumbnail art direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2018–2023 | Dated DevForum portfolios for Jailbreak, Miner's Haven, Ghost Simulator, Fishing Legends and other games show artist-built action scenes, individually staged character renders, lighting by genre and some title-led covers. The External portfolio includes work dated 2022. | Use older examples for big, recognisable shapes, staged poses and cohesive lighting. Classic Roblox styling can look deliberate at high finish. |
| 2024 | Roblox rolled out 16:9 Home recommendation tiles and launched thumbnail personalization. Roblox also announced less recommendation and search visibility for metadata closely resembling existing experiences. | Keep the requested mechanic truthful and recognisable. Rebuild reference techniques into a composition distinctive to the user's game. |
| 2025 | Roblox rolled the widescreen Home treatment out to new mobile/tablet users and updated thumbnail personalization to preserve traffic to an existing winner when testing new art. | Make the core action legible on mobile. When variants are requested, vary a clear visual hook while holding the actual game promise steady. |
| 2026 | Roblox announced that Home recommendations require a custom thumbnail from 19 October. Roblox documentation distinguishes the Home thumbnail set from the experience detail-page gallery. November charts and creator examples show clean game-like compositions alongside illustrated, cinematic, vehicle-led and restrained horror art. | Use a genre-matched benchmark instead of treating higher gloss, stronger saturation or more particles as universal quality improvements. |

## Research sources and access

- [Roblox Charts](https://www.roblox.com/charts) is the public discovery snapshot. The November capture follows all pages returned by the public Explore API for the recorded session and filters. The public thumbnail API supplied available experience gallery images and icons. The October base, its source IDs and its annotations remain intact.
- X was checked through public creator profiles and their attached photographs. Verified posts met 1,500 views at capture. A portfolio collage counts as one attachment. Search windows and public-profile availability limit coverage. View counts are exposure for a post.
- [BuiltByBit commissions](https://builtbybit.com/threads/741153/), [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/koolako/make-you-a-cool-looking-gfx) and [Codartt](https://codartt.com/explore/graphic-design) provide seller or creator samples. [RoHire](https://rohire.dev/listings/260580) surfaced commission offerings through search but previews were unavailable at direct retrieval. [BuiltByBit Roblox resources](https://builtbybit.com/resources/roblox/), [ClearlyDev](https://clearlydev.com/product/shop-ui) and the [Roblox Creator Store](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) provide context on asset storefronts. UI kit previews, price sheets, isolated UGC catalogue images and generic marketing tiles are excluded from the generative thumbnail reference set.
- Historical portfolios are dated by their public post unless the artist labels an individual work with a date. Artists may edit portfolios. A portfolio post date is evidence of the listing, and the reported chart date identifies the visibility snapshot. Neither inherently dates each underlying image.
- A rotating personalised Home feed, inactive Home variants, private or deleted X content and paywalled portfolio material were outside the public capture. Chart placement, concurrent players, post views and seller claims do not validate image-specific CTR or QPTR.

## Match the craft before delivery

1. Choose one close reference for the game's visual hook and another for execution. Open their actual images from the catalogue and give each a specific job. If a reference is a comparison board, inspect the relevant tile and select a single-scene example for framing.
2. Preserve the requested avatars and approved face assets. Compare apparent character size, lens height, perspective, pose, eye line, contact points, edge separation, palette, materials and background density. Match those traits at the level appropriate to the genre.
3. Show the game's actual interaction with a distinct arrangement. Borrow craft choices from several examples. Keep the user’s own character, mechanic, set and branding in control. Leave text off by default and keep essential action clear of the lower overlay area.
4. Compare the draft with the selected examples at full size and reduced to approximately 320 × 180. In the smaller preview, check that the same focal action survives. At full size check hand-to-prop contact, coherent shadows, consistent textures, edge halos, text and Roblox anatomy. Edit material shortcomings with the smallest targeted revision available.

| Treatment | Reviewed examples | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clean traversal and obby | CRT-047, CRT-078, CRT-083, CRT-163 | One clear movement axis, visible contact and a readable next platform. Let path geometry explain the challenge. |
| Simulator and reward reveal | CRT-051, CRT-056, CRT-175, CRT-178 | Size and placement explain the reward or upgrade. Cluster secondary props and keep one dominant focal action. |
| Cinematic action and tower defence | CRT-004, CRT-007, CRT-008, CRT-009, CRT-147 | Rank hero, opponent and backdrop by silhouette, exposure and depth. Put effect highlights around actionable forms. |
| Restrained horror | CRT-027, CRT-077, CRT-180 | Use one light cue, a readable threat and a deliberate sightline. Choose darkness levels at phone size. |
| Vehicles and environments | CRT-010, CRT-011, CRT-012, CRT-174 | Use purposeful camera height, recognisable shape language, grounded contact and consistent reflections. |
| Social and roleplay | CRT-018, CRT-095 | Group faces and poses around a believable shared activity. Use focused warm lighting and keep decorative clutter secondary. |
